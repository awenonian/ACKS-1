# Quellus Tarcalus

The toll-taker at the Mirmen crossing. **Not yet in play.** Styles himself
*Quellus Tarcalus Limitanus* — "of the frontier," a cognomen he awarded himself.

## VOICE

- "The commission does not expire. The man who could relieve me of it has not come."
- "You've read the stone. Good. Then you know where you are standing."
- "I have held this crossing for four years with eleven men and no letters."

**Can't say:** that he is stealing. He has no vocabulary for it. He also cannot
say the word *abandoned* about himself, and will substitute *unrelieved* every time.

## INTENTION

- **What they're for:** the antagonist who is not a bandit. He is what the Lawful
  position looks like when it goes bad while staying sincere, and he must never be
  written as a hypocrite. He is the argument that the law is whoever is still
  standing where the law used to be.
- **What they want:** to hold the Mirmen crossing, be paid for holding it, and be
  recognised as having held it. He genuinely believes he is the last piece of the
  Empire on this road.
- **What they'd never do:** take a bribe framed as a bribe. Bribery will bounce off
  him unless it is dressed as a fee, a tariff, or a fine — at which point he will
  take it without noticing. *(Ruling: Edward's Bribery power works on him only if
  the player frames it in the language of a levy. Worth flagging if it comes up.)*

## POSITIONS

Nothing committed in play yet.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** he moved the boundary stone. He did it in the second year, with six
  men and a sledge, and he remembers the exact date.
- **Believes:** that moving it *restored* a border the Empire had let slip, and
  that a proper survey would vindicate him. He is wrong, and he has never checked.
- **Lies about:** his commission's date. He will show it; the seal is genuine; the
  term ran out three years ago and he will cover the line with his thumb.
- **Won't say:** how many of the eleven men are still his and how many are just
  living there now.

## Facts

- Held a genuine imperial commission as a customs officer of the Borderlands. It
  expired. Nobody came to renew or relieve it, because the legions went west.
- He has collected the Empire's half on treasure recovered in a strip of land that
  is legally *outside* the Empire, by moving the marker outward. He forwards a
  portion to an imperial office that no longer answers, and keeps the rest to pay
  his men.
