Read `notes/gm/STATE.md` first. Everything true about the stone, the nine markers,
Quellus, and Nauriva is resolved there. Do not re-decide any of it.

## Where we left off

Four hours before dawn, camped at the fifth stone, a day short of Mirmen Ford. The
player ended the session mid-inference and said explicitly that he wanted time to
work out what he and Edward think. **Do not open by summarising his options or
nudging him toward a reading.** Ask for the recap, then let him move first.

## What is true and must not drift

- The Auran inscription is **genuine**. Every forgery test came back clean and will
  keep coming back clean. If he tests it again, it is still genuine.
- The stone was **moved one league east**. It had fallen; Quellus re-erected it.
- Nothing lives in the moved strip. Its value is what Auran treasure law says about
  ground outside the Empire, in a country full of ruins. **He has the law. He has not
  connected it. That connection is his to make and I do not make it for him.**
- Nauriva is Quellus's, four years running, paid to report cargo ahead of arrival.

## What is prepped and ready to be walked into

- **The road east.** The leg from this stone to the next should run one league short.
  This is a testable prediction he has the numbers for, on the road he is already
  taking. If he paces it, it is short. No throw.
- **The empty socket**, one league back west, off the road, with a stone-shaped
  depression. Costs a ride back. He has not thought of it yet; do not offer it.
- **Mirmen Ford**, and Quellus Tarcalus. See his character file. He is sincere, he is
  not a hypocrite, and he cannot recognise a bribe as a bribe — it works dressed as a
  fee, a tariff or a fine, and insults him otherwise.
- **Nauriva is already there**, and Quellus has known since roughly midnight that a
  scholar was digging at the stone. Clock at 2 of 4.

## Pressure, if he stalls

Do not force the ford. Tick a clock instead: Quellus's men come *out* to the stone,
politely, to ask what the digging was about. That moves the confrontation onto the
road, where Edward has a crossbow, 140 feet of range, and four hit points.

## The thing to hold back

The nine stones fence a road into the Waste, sealed behind the last sorcerer-king by
his own people because they were afraid of him. That is the campaign, not this
session. Feed it a fragment at a time and only when the antiquary side needs work
that isn't about tolls.
