## Proficiencies

- **Magical Engineering** (class) — recognise conventional magic items or special components
  by examining them 1 turn, throw **11+**. Extract components from constructs. +1 magic
  research, +5% rate. *Cannot* recognise unique items, discern command words, spot cursed or
  trapped items, or read charges remaining.
- **Knowledge (history)** (general) — recall expert commentary or information, throw **11+**.
  Can make a living as an expert. Rank 1 of 3.
- **Driving** (from Expert Traveling) — control 6 droving animals; double road movement bonus
  with vehicles.
- **Adventuring** (free to all) — camp, cook, first aid, ride outside combat, rough valuation
  of coin, goods, gems, jewelry, magic and monsters. Dungeonbashing 18+, Climbing 8+.
  *Note: STR −1 makes his Dungeonbashing throw effectively 22+ — he cannot force a stuck door.*

## Class powers (Venturer)

- **Bribery** — +1 reaction for a day's pay to the target, +2 for a week's, +3 for a month's.
  Politely deniable; only chargeable as a crime on an unmodified 2d6 roll of 2.
- **Diplomacy** — +1 on all reaction rolls when parleying with intelligent creatures.
- **Expert Bargaining** — buys 10% cheaper, sells 10% dearer. Against another bargainer,
  opposed reaction rolls decide who gets the discount.
- **Expert Traveling** — Driving (taken).
- **Mercantile Network** — in a market he has entered before, treat it as one class larger
  (Class I stays I) *or* take +1 market impact, whichever helps more.
- **Multilingual** — three bonus languages (taken, below).
- **Pathfinding** — +4 to avoid getting lost in familiar territory: at 1st level, the settled
  areas of his starting region plus one 24-mile hex out from settled land. Radius grows one
  hex per level.
- **Treachery** — on achieving a Friendly reaction via Diplomacy or Bribery, he may attack
  treacherously; everything within 30' rolls surprise at **−3**. Party exempt only with a
  prearranged signal. Does not work if the opposition's negotiator also has Treachery.

**Henchmen:** up to **5** (4 + CHA mod). Henchman loyalty modified by +1.

## Languages

Speaks **Krysean** (native) and **Common Auran** (lingua franca of the Empire).
Multilingual's three: **Classical Auran** · **Classical Krysean** · **Zaharan**.
Literate in all five (INT 9 clears the illiteracy line at 8).
