## Session 1

**Character creation.** Edward Copper — born Uxentio Kupratar, of Kupra in Krysea —
Venturer 1 on the Antiquary template, Lawful, 22 years old. Attributes rolled with
the 5d6 on Charisma and 4d6 on Intellect and Dexterity; Charisma came up 12 and was
floored to 13, Dexterity came up 16, so he ended quicker than he is charming. Hit
points rolled 3, raised to 4 by the minimum. Four hit points.

Lines and veils settled before play: no violence to children, no body horror,
torture veiled. The player was told about the stop/skip/rewind lever.

The Antiquary template was taken by choice rather than rolled, at the player's
request, as a one-time chargen exception.

**The boundary stone on the Mirmen road.** Six days out from Kavala with Destrio
Mennicus's caravan, hired to read boundary stones. The fifth stone of the day was
wrong: it cited eleven leagues to Casra Nuvicerre where Edward's own road notes said
twelve, and though it was of the reign of Ulkyreus like the other four, the chisel
marks were still sharp under a thumb. Edward told Destrio quietly and left the
decision to him. Destrio checked the cut himself, made camp, gave up the ford
crossing, and asked for a written statement a magistrate could read.

**Nauriva left.** Forty minutes into the dig, her mule and her blanket roll were gone
and the road east was empty. She left eleven days' wages on Destrio's board. Destrio
refused to speculate about why. Edward chose not to chase her.

**Under the stone.** Edward finished the dig by firelight. The socket is a separate
block, not granite, and the Auran marker does not fit it — it is wedged plumb with
packed rubble and broken tile that is not old. The block's upper face, sheltered under
the granite's overhang, carries Zaharan carving: an interdiction, not a boundary; one
of a series of at least nine; a house-name worn down to `BET-ASHUR—`. Edward dated it
to roughly 655 BE, the last generation of Zahar. Two markers in one hole, nine hundred
years apart.
